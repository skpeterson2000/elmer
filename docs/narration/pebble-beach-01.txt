PEBBLE BEACH GOLF LINKS - THE FIRST - narration for a voice
(Technician course. Read plainly, at a walking pace. Full stops are pauses.
The figures are the game's card, which is the real card; nothing here is
in the game that is not in this text, and nothing here is invented.)

--- THE HOLE (read once, at the tee, before the first player is addressed) ---

The first at Pebble Beach.
Par four. Three hundred and seventy-seven yards.

A gentle opener. The fairway bends a little to the right, and the sea breeze
is behind you here - the ocean holes come later. A good drive rides it.

Two things to know. A fairway bunker sits on the right, two hundred and
thirty yards out, and it catches the drive that drifts. And the green is
guarded on the left, close in, by sand - so the second shot favors the
right half.

The green is small. Twenty-eight yards deep. Take enough club, and no more.

A four here is a good start. Nobody wins the round on the first.

--- THE ADDRESS (read before each stroke; the game fills in the name, the club and the lie) ---

[Name] addresses the ball. The [club] in hand. [Yards] to go, from the [lie].

    Examples, the way the game says them:
    Scott addresses the ball. The driver in hand. Three hundred and seventy-seven to go, from the tee.
    Halyard addresses the ball. The iron in hand. One hundred and twenty-two to go, from the fairway.
    Scott addresses the ball. The putter in hand. On the green.

--- THE CUE (a short sound, or one word, when the question comes up) ---

Your shot.

--- THE CALLS (read one, after the answer, before the answers are shown) ---

On the fairway:
    Pured it.
    Right down the middle.
    That'll play.

On the green:
    On the dance floor.
    Stuck it.
    That's looking at it.

Through the green:
    Flew the green.
    Too much club.
    Airmailed it.

Holed:
    In the hole!
    Drained it.
    Bottom of the cup.

A hole in one:
    A hole in one!

Short, into the rough:
    Topped it.
    Fat. Chunked it.
    Skied that one.

Into the sand:
    Sliced it into the sand.
    Pulled it into the bunker.
    Beach.

Into the water:
    Hooked it into the water.
    Wet.
    That's a splash. What was the wind?

A missed putt:
    Lipped out.
    Left it short.
    Burned the edge.

--- THE STROKE, IN WORDS (the game composes these; examples for the voice's cadence) ---

Driver. Two hundred and fifty-five yards. Fairway. One hundred and twenty-two to go.
Driver. A foul ball. Into the fairway bunker.
Iron. One hundred and twenty-two yards. On the green. Nine feet.
Putt holed. Three, for a birdie.
Wedge, from the sand. A foul ball. Short, and into the rough.

--- THE CARD (read when the hole is done) ---

That's the first. [Name], [score]; [Name], [score].
    Scores, as the game names them: an eagle, a birdie, par, a bogey, a double bogey, a triple bogey.

On to the second.
