Standing

Every title is earned inside the class it names — there is no route to a General title that does not run through General questions. The upper three steps rest on mock exam results, and that evidence goes stale the way a licence does: current for {{ rank_rules.current_days }} days, then a {{ rank_rules.grace_days - rank_rules.current_days }}-day grace period where one passing exam renews it, then it must be earned again. Sustained practice at the tier's own standard keeps it current without re-sitting anything — a few questions a week genuinely protects proficiency, and the bar rises with the title you are holding.

These are ELMER's own study ranks, not licences. They are earned against ELMER's copy of the question pools and grant no operating privileges of any kind. Only the FCC issues an amateur or commercial licence, and only a real exam session in front of accredited VEs or a COLEM leads to one.
