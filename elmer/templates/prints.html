A card in the spare space

Most sheets end part way down their last page. That space can carry one of the cards the waiting screens show — a quotation, a piece of the history — with its source beneath it. A card goes on only where one fits whole; it never pushes the sheet onto another page, and a gap too small to look meant is left empty.

ELMER’s choice leans to the sheet: the gear on an antenna sheet, operating on a band chart, famous hams on a parks and summits list. With your own picks, a sheet none of them fits goes out without one. The full band chart, antenna sheets and parks and summits lists take a card; the one-page band chart, the RF exposure record and certificates do not.
