{# The door. The only page in ELMER that assumes nothing about who is here. It deliberately does not extend base.html: that shell carries the operator chip, the rank strip and the account menu, all of which are somebody's, and drawing them for a browser that has not said who it is was the whole problem. ELMER used to fall back to the first account on the unit, so the corner showed a name nobody had chosen - and on a sealed account it showed that name while signed out, which is how a licensed operator came to find the program treating them as though they held nothing and telling them it did not know where they were. A name here is not a credential.
